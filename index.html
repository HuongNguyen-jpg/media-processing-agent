Media Processing AI Assistant

Turn media links and files into organized insight: extract what is available, surface recurring themes, and prepare reports that are easy to review and archive.

01 / PURPOSEWhat this assistant does

Accept an audio/video link or file, extract accessible content and metadata, assess prominence, group related information, and prepare a Word summary plus an Excel tracklist for storage and review.

03 / WORKFLOWFrom media to report

Receive & identifyCapture the link/file, request, source, and supplied metadata.
Inspect available contentRead the description, tracklist, and lyrics when accessible.
Analyze themesIdentify emotional keywords, recurring ideas, and their frequency.
Assess prominenceRate impact and explain the evidence behind the assessment.
Group & deduplicateOrganize the 16 songs into four themes and merge repeated ideas.
Validate boundariesFlag missing, conflicting, or unverifiable information.
Prepare deliverablesStructure a grouped tracklist and a concise message summary.
Review & hand offRecord the source and limitations; deliver files for human review.

06 / TRUST & LIMITSWhat it can and cannot do

✓ Can support

  • Organize provided metadata and tracklists.
  • Analyze descriptions and lyrics that are accessible.
  • Suggest themes, recurring ideas, and prominence.
  • Prepare structured content for Word and Excel.

! Must not assume

  • Invent song titles, lyrics, view counts, or sources.
  • Claim to verify content that could not be accessed.
  • Present uncertain or conflicting data as fact.
  • Replace human review of the final report.
Implementation note: This page describes the agent workflow. Live extraction from YouTube, lyric access, and Word/Excel file generation require suitable integrations and permissions. Unavailable content must be labeled as unverified.
